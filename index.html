A little about us

From Bangalore
to Melbourne, to Goa

We both grew up in Bangalore before life took us to Melbourne — where, somewhere between Sunday markets and long evenings, we found each other. After years of adventures together, we're coming home to celebrate our wedding with the people we love most.

We chose Prainha because it felt exactly right: a private beach cove on the Arabian Sea, the kind of place that feels like it was made for sunsets and dancing. We cannot wait to share it with you.

With all our love, Malaika & Rishabh
