Cultural sensitisation

Cultural sensitivity is foundational for cohesive, inclusive teams. Understanding diverse perspectives lifts communication, innovation, and problem-solving. The programme used interactive workshops, real-life scenarios, and continuous learning to help teams collaborate more effectively and create an inclusive work environment that leverages diversity.

Challenges

  • Surfacing and addressing unconscious bias with clear business outcomes
  • Techniques and key factors for building strong cross-cultural relationships

Outcome

  • Deeper understanding of cultural differences and how to navigate them
  • Communication adapted to diverse styles, lifting cohesion and reducing conflict
  • Consistent inclusive practices applied across the organisation

They focus on transforming the quality of life in urban India by partnering closely with citizens and government institutions. The brief was to establish strong policies, develop an efficient HR platform, build a robust performance evaluation system, and identify and grow high-potential employees.

What we built

  • HR policies designed around employee well-being and satisfaction, fully aligned with statutory regulations and the realities of NGO operations
  • A recommended HRMS platform to manage operational efficiency, ensure compliance, support data-driven decisions, and improve the employee experience
  • A resilient performance management framework using SMART goals and consistent assessments to foster development and tie individual performance to organisational goals
  • Individual Development Plans built into the review process to guide subsequent development initiatives
  • A process to identify high-potential employees for career advancement by capturing behavioural competencies
